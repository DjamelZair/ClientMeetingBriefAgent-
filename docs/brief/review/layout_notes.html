The critique, and what answers it

No summary; the gist is spread over chips and stepsA summary strip: identity with age, income, meeting date and care, then four stat cards (segment, tenure, review, held back), each with a value, a label and a status word with its glyph.
The left column competes with the stageThe column is gone. The wallet moved into step 1's part, as a plain list with hairlines instead of tinted rows.
What not to offer is easy to missA held back card in the summary names the products, the part gets a cherry top rule and a cherry title. With nothing held back both stay quiet: a grey "Nothing held back".
Five equal columnsLane widths follow content (1.1, 1.25, 1.05, 1, 1.3), and parts end where their content ends instead of stretching.
Repeated textSteps 1 and 4 drop their Returned line: the part right under them says the same words. Steps 2, 3 and 5 keep it because it adds counts.
The step to brief link is thin, lost on a phoneEach part sits directly under its step with a stem from the marker; a rail joins the markers; hover or focus in a lane lights marker, stem and part; a click still pins it. On a phone each part stays under its step.
A step feeds more than one part without saying soEach part heading carries the numbered marker of its step; the summary cards carry theirs (click selects the step and lights everything it made).

  • Spacing 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48
  • Radius 0 on cards, buttons and inputs; 2px on small badges; square step markers
  • Depth: 1px hairline, surface change on hover, no shadow
  • Tabs: selected gets ink, 600 and a 2px accent underline
  • Top nav 48; button padding 12 16; compact controls at the utility bar's 32; touch 48; 2px focus outline
  • Sentence case labels
  • IBM Blue and the greys: the palette is the user's

  • Weight 300 display: nothing here is 42px or larger

The six hard rules

1. The agent prepares, the advisor decidesMet. No layout element is an action to sell: the only buttons are replay, the bar, the answers, the toggles and copy. The headline keeps "Worth discussing" and "Worth asking about"; the summary speaks of held back items and reviews.
2. Synthetic fields stay labelledMet. The review card label reads "Periodic review, synthetic date"; the review part ends with "Synthetic date."; the timeline says "synthetic"; the footer says review dates and vulnerability flags are synthetic.
3. No raw probabilityMet. The ranked component is not mounted. The summary shows counts, dates and words only; scores appear as bands ("Scores 50% to 100%") and take up rates, as before.
4. Held back stays visible and explainedMet. Named in the summary card and in the headline sub line, listed with rule and reason in part 3, marked in the wallet with glyph and word; nothing is collapsed.
5. Every number from data.jsonMet. The HTML holds only words and mounts. Counts, dates, tenure and step numbers come from the components reading data.json once through field.js.
6. The step to part link gets clearerMet. Part under step on every width, a stem and a rail, hover and focus light the pair, the part heading carries the step marker, replay fades each part until its step is done.

Round 2: what changed, and which report asked for it

Layout (critique, user research)

  • Products and the timeline left part 1 for their own row under parts 1 to 4, tagged as from step 1 with a short stem up into part 1; parts 1 to 4 now share one height, so no lane leaves a hole
  • Spine columns 1, 0.95, 0.95, 1.05, 1.65: no answer group stacks one per row and no part title wraps at 1440
  • The follow up line sits at the top of part 5; the band chart moved behind each item's Why
  • The part number sits left of the title, on the stem's side

Wording and the summary (user research, critique)

  • Step 2 returns the count only ("4 products scored")
  • A held back item shows no score on its face; the band is behind Why, said to be the model's
  • "Nothing to discuss after the checks."; the question kind "Top suggestion" reads "Highest after the checks"
  • The user's decision: a product the client owns is "Has it" (mapped in variants.js; data.json still says "Held"). Owned products sit in one group; held back rows are tinted and ruled apart
  • The summary drops the repeated segment and adds "What changed"; every card carries a focusable step tag; a calm glyph when nothing is held back
  • One context line under the header; the data source is read from data.json

Design system (design_system.md)

  • Group A: skeleton bars on a hairline token (the Mercury skeleton shows again), one held back rule for all layers, a single lit ring, swatches painted from each scheme's tokens, contrast.py now scans variants.js
  • Group C: five role sizes (caption, body-sm, body, title, headline) plus a phone headline; no 10 or 11px literals; line height tokens; Mercury titles 480, Linear titles 500
  • Group D (cheap part): a 2px step, size, border and motion tokens; the js reads easing and durations from tokens.css
  • Mercury: white cards with a 1px rule and tinted items; selected tabs take the soft tint instead of the solid pill
  • ibm borrows linear's zero tracking on 12 and 14px text, flagged as a deviation
  • Groups B (colour tidy) and E (renames, dead code) are left for later: safe but wide

Phone: summary first, each step above its part, products folded behind one button that counts what is inside; "What not to offer" starts about 300px higher.
